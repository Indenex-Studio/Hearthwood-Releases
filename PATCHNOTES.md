# Hearthwood patch notes

What changed, newest first, one section per day. Plain words, no build numbers. A day marked **WORLD WIPE** needs a fresh world; the launcher always asks before it wipes yours.

## 18 October

> **WORLD WIPE.** The land is reshaped with mountains, big hills, caves and a new marsh, so this update needs a fresh world. The launcher asks first, and "Keep my world" is the safe choice if you are not ready.

**World**
- Big hills everywhere and tall mountains in the highland. Steep slopes slow you down but never stop you.
- Caves are cut into hillsides, with a stone floor and real darkness inside. Bring a torch.
- A marsh near the start, with reeds, peat and gentle mud that slows you a little.
- Grass reaches further out, following your view distance, and lies low around berry and hazel bushes so they stand out.
- Fireflies no longer hover over the open sea.
- Far painted mountains on the horizon, clearer far land in fair weather, painted water and clouds, and soft rain that leaves a low mist in the hollows.

**Crafting**
- Burning-stick torch. Plant a lit one in the ground as a standing light.
- Drying pots show how long is left and what helps while you wait. Under a roof, drying clay ignores the rain, and only its maker can pick it up in the open.
- A small kiln for firing a few pieces at a time.
- Clay work has new hand motions, and you lift and look at a fired pot when you pick it up.

**Survival**
- If you die, you keep your belt tools and worn gear. Only your bag drops.
- A shared pot at the hearth: anyone in the owner's group can ladle a bowl.
- A fire beside a lean-to keeps the cold off. The warning for cold says how long is left, and the warmth ring breathes when little warmth remains.
- A fire waits for the person who lit it if they are offline.

**Animals**
- Carry hens and rabbits whole and cut them up at a fire or table. Pick up live ones too.
- Feed hens, rabbits and sheep by hand to tame them. Tame animals follow you, stay put when told, and take names.
- The pen: a ring of fence that holds animals. Animal care rank 25 unlocks its gate.
- A tame pair in a closed pen can have young.

**Building**
- Timber-framed cottage, a step up from clay, with working shutters.
- Walk-in clay hut with a tamped clay floor and windows with board shutters.
- A bracken bed in the lean-to, a taller lean-to, and building sites that tell you how to make what they need.
- Hang a door from the right-click menu, and take down a finished build from it too.
- A build's name note says who built it ("Bench, built by Mara").

**Sailing**
- Log raft, and a plank boat with a reed sail. Wind matters, and a boat that is going fast looks it.
- Right-click a boat for its menu. Seamanship shows in the sail, and the map shows the wind.
- Boats make their own sounds on the water.
- The plank boat's sail lets out square to the wind, and you sit at the helm. The reed sail fills out near top speed.

**Playing together**
- Share your home with a friend from the right-click menu on that person.
- Right-click menus on doors, fires, hearths, baskets, chests, jars, kilns, lamps, troughs, drying clay, drying racks, snares, benches and rest spots. They replace the V, Shift+E and Z keys.
- Sit and lie down are on the emote wheel now, so H is free.
- The key list (F1) shows every right-click menu.
- At rank 50, Potter and Builder choose a focus to specialise in.
- Kit and Capling join Pebble and Bramble. All four have smooth bodies, painted heads and more look choices.

**Look and sound**
- Pines grow needle clumps, giant pines look fuller, and thatch roofs are shaded.
- The wool tunic is remade as a small crumpled T with a soft cord belt.
- Tools are carried tilted, with a relaxed swing as you walk.
- Gorse looks right again, with no floating spikes or yellow beads.
- Your woven back basket shows on your back, and other players see it too.
- Different music in each area and at dawn.

**Launcher**
- The game is now called Hearthwood. The launcher reads new builds from its new home.
- The launcher has a "Patch notes" link that opens this page.

**Fixes**
- Esc opens the menu again every time.
- A boiled egg can be eaten. Trees fade only when they hide your body.
- The game and server run lighter: grass detail levels, Medium graphics preset lighter than High.
- The journal has an item book: linked boxes show what goes into what.
- Skills have their own page on K. Words across the game are plainer.

## 17 October

**World**
- Biomes: a rocky highland with flint, slate and copper ore, a marsh with reeds and peat, and a sea coast.
- The warm, Ghibli-style meadow ground is the default everywhere. Trees and rocks come from the new nature kit, in our colours.
- Fallen giant logs, lone flowers, butterflies, pollen, fireflies by the water and dragonflies.
- Felled trees fall away from you, leave a stump and slowly regrow.
- Morning frost, heavy winter snow, a time-of-day arc on the sundial, and 3D water with reflections.

**Crafting**
- Bows and arrows. Boiled eggs, fletched arrows, a waterskin and a spade that digs faster than a stick.
- Maker's marks: pots and many made things say who made them, and you can pick a mark and decorate with slip.
- Clay troughs, roof tiles and a stone-ringed hearth for winter nights.
- A picture menu for crafting and for building.

**Survival**
- Stamina is checked by the server. Wild roots, hazelnuts and mushrooms to find.
- A signal fire from fiber sends a thick smoke column. Felled trees give sticks and fiber.
- After a death you wake in the open, with a faint light pointing back to where you fell.
- Lie down on your mat. Rest spots offer to set your waking place.

**Animals**
- Hens that lay eggs and roost in a coop at night. A wild rabbit and a rabbit snare.
- Deer bed down through the middle of the day. Animals shy away when you splash through water.

**Building**
- Homes are 25% bigger, with real doorways. A brick house with a roof of fired clay tiles.
- A wooden chest, a straw bed, later a rabbit fur bed, and a sundial.
- Homes name who built them, and friends who finish one together get a house-warming.

**Playing together**
- Towns with signboards, offices, voting, bans and a shared store and upkeep.
- A friends list on O, groups, "show the way" to a friend, and a right-click menu on players.
- Emotes on the Q wheel: wave, cheer, dance, bow, clap, shrug and point.
- Choose your name, your race and your look when you start. Several characters per login.
- Hold the Use key to repeat an action. Hotbar slots on 1 to 0, and the tool in your hand is ringed.

**Look and sound**
- Warm paper windows with a rounded friendly font, bouncy menus and a soft "Waking up" veil.
- Painted look for props, houses, pots, animals and trees. Chibi Pebble and Bramble, with blinking and expressions.
- Real recorded music, and many new sounds: chopping, trees falling, doors, hens, kilns, simmering pots and roasting.

**Launcher**
- A Player launcher without server parts, a Refresh button, resizable windows, and a "What's new" panel.

**Fixes**
- F8 sends a bug report that opens an issue by itself. /stuck frees you if you are trapped.
- The server refuses walking through fences, walls, trunks and boulders.
- Lots of smoothing: world building on worker threads, cheaper animals, fewer hitches.

## 16 October

> **WORLD WIPE.** The land was reshaped with hills, a winding stream and ponds. This was the first planned wipe.

**World**
- Hills, a stream you can wade, ponds, groves and glades, heath on hilltops and birch wetland by the water. Landmarks and trails, each one different.
- Day and night with seasons, cold and snow, thunderstorms, starry nights, rainbows and valley mist.

**Crafting**
- Crafting stations and the crafting table. Better tools work faster and wear out, and the repair table fixes them.
- Craft times follow your skill, and one shared skill curve is used everywhere. Masters work by heart.
- Cooking pot and Potter's bench, storage jar, kiln, clay oil lamp, roof tiles, and batch devices for clay, grit and tempering.

**Survival**
- Running and stamina, breath you can see in the cold, new-arrival warmth, snow that wets you.
- Thirst eases in a new character's first hour.

**Animals**
- Sheep give wool; spin it into yarn and weave cloth. Leather from a drying rack, leather foot wraps and a wool tunic.
- Red deer at the woodland edges, with hunting and sounds. A Stalking skill and sneak-attack hits.

**Building**
- Stick fence and gate, raised hearth, log cabin, mud-brick house with a hearth, and see-through shelters.
- Claim fences with gates for members only.

**Playing together**
- Trading between players. Name your camp. Whisper and shout.
- Equipment panel, bag with drag and drop, and a hotbar. Jump with Space, use things with E.

**Launcher**
- A one-button launcher with automatic releases, self-update and server start.

## 15 October

**World**
- The first playable world: a forest with a painted, moody look, day and night, weather and a living soundscape.

**Crafting**
- Tinder, hand-drill fire, bow drill, stone knife, digging stick, baskets, cordage and lashing, chopping with a stone axe.
- Clay from the riverbank to a fired pot of water, with a kiln you build together.

**Survival**
- Hunger, thirst, warmth, getting wet, death and respawn. A carrying limit.

**Animals**
- Sheep flocks: shear them for wool, hunt them, and get leather.

**Building**
- Lean-to and clay hut built together in stages, hut doors and storage baskets.

**Playing together**
- Chat by distance, land claims, PvP rules, and a journal with skills that remembers what you have learned.
